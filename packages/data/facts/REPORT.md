# Arsenals and energy

Generated by `pnpm --filter @empire/data facts` from the sources below. The statistics pages show these figures; no rule reads them. A territory sums the figures of its statistics codes (see `canon.yaml`); — means none of them has one.

## Sources

- `activePersonnel`: Global Firepower 2025, active personnel
- `tanks`: Global Firepower 2025, combat tanks
- `combatAircraft`: Global Firepower 2025, fighters, interceptors and attack aircraft
- `navalShips`: Global Firepower 2025, total naval assets
- `oilTwh`: Our World in Data (Energy Institute; The Shift Project), oil production
- `gasTwh`: Our World in Data (Energy Institute; The Shift Project), natural gas production
- `electricityTwh`: Our World in Data (Ember; Energy Institute), electricity generation

- Global Firepower (globalfirepower.com), 2025 edition, via the nupurmadaan04/unified-military-analytics scrape on GitHub.
- Our World in Data energy dataset (CC BY 4.0), from the Energy Institute Statistical Review of World Energy, Ember and The Shift Project.

Global Firepower: https://raw.githubusercontent.com/nupurmadaan04/unified-military-analytics/b3d04fb5e514f1182957b37f55bc70037a8ebf80/data/military_cleaned.csv

Our World in Data: https://raw.githubusercontent.com/owid/energy-data/f8de88a4f6f9cab44f1cf606047cbd6c4414042c/owid-energy-data.csv
